Sample project workflow

Clarity is part of the craft.

A reusable contractor process that explains decisions, responsibilities, and communication—without inventing schedules, warranties, or guarantees.

01 · FIT

Initial project review

Confirm project type, location, budget range, decision-makers, desired timing, and whether the opportunity fits the contractor's real service model.

02 · SITE

Site consultation

Inspect existing conditions and access. A provider gate prevents the demo from implying a visit has been booked.

03 · SCOPE

Design and selections

Define drawings, allowances, products, trade responsibilities, owner-supplied items, and unresolved conditions.

04 · AGREE

Agreement and schedule

Use the client's actual contract, payment schedule, cancellation terms, permits, warranty, and communication plan.

05 · BUILD

Construction updates

Set the real update cadence, site-access rules, dust-control plan, work hours, and change-order approvals.

06 · CLOSE

Walkthrough and closeout

Document completion, punch items, manuals, care guidance, final approvals, and only the warranties actually offered.

Expectation setting

What the client version must define.

  • Actual design responsibilities and outside partners
  • Permitting, inspection, and engineering roles
  • Deposits, progress payments, and change authorization
  • Site access, work hours, pets, dust, and temporary utilities
  • Communication cadence and issue escalation
  • Verified warranty and service terms
